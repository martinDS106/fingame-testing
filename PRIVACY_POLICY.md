# Privacy Policy — Fin-Game (MVP)

**Last updated:** 2026-04-22

This Privacy Policy explains how Fin-Game ("we", "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Fin-Game mobile application (the "App").

## 1) What we collect

### Account information
- Email address (for login)
- Basic profile fields you provide (display name, avatar)

### App activity
- Progress and completion data (courses, lessons, quizzes, simulations)
- Points/coins and XP totals and logs
- Quiz attempts and scores

### Optional content
- Profile photo (if you choose to upload one)

### Device/network
- We may receive technical information needed to run the App (e.g., errors). We do not intentionally collect precise location.

## 2) How we use information
- Create and manage your account
- Sync your progress and points across devices
- Provide learning content, quizzes, simulations, and leaderboard
- Prevent abuse and secure the platform (admin moderation)
- Improve reliability and performance

## 3) How we share information
- **Service provider (Supabase):** We use Supabase for authentication, database, and storage. Your app data is stored in our Supabase project.
- **Leaderboard:** We display a top-10 leaderboard with limited public fields (display name, avatar, points).
- **No selling of personal data:** We do not sell your personal data.

## 4) Data retention
We keep your account and in-app data while your account is active, and as needed to provide the App. You may request deletion (see below).

## 5) Security
We use access controls and database row-level security to limit data access. No system is 100% secure; please use a strong password.

## 6) Children
Fin-Game is intended for general audiences. If you are under the minimum age required to consent in your country, please use the App with parental guidance.

## 7) Your choices
- You can update profile info in the App.
- You can request account deletion by contacting us.

## 8) Contact
If you have questions or requests (including deletion), contact:
- **Email:** support@fin-game.app (replace with your real support email)
