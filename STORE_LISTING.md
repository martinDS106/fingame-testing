## App Name
Fin-Game

## Short description (EN)
Learn money skills through videos, quizzes, and simulations — earn points and climb the leaderboard.

## Full description (EN)
Fin-Game is a gamified financial learning app built for young learners in Egypt.

Learn → Simulate → Earn Points

### What you can do
- Watch short financial learning videos
- Take quizzes and track your score
- Try a simplified investment simulator with virtual money
- Earn points/coins and level up
- Compete on a global leaderboard
- Switch language: Arabic / English

### Notes
- The simulator uses virtual money only and is for educational purposes.
- Points/coins have no cash value.
